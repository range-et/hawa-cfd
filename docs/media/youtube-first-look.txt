Title
Hawa: GPU flow simulation in Rhino and Grasshopper

Description
Hawa runs a lattice Boltzmann solver inside Rhino 8. Define the case in Grasshopper. View the velocity and pressure in Rhino.

This video presents captions and screenshots from the verified Rhino sessions. The vortex run completed 200 iterations on PyTorch MPS.
