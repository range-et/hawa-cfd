Title
Hawa: 3D block flow in Grasshopper with MPS

Description
This Hawa example connects a domain, closed geometry, physics, and boundaries to a lattice Boltzmann solver inside Rhino 8.

The video presents captions and screenshots from the verified Rhino session. The case completed 300 MPS iterations on a 33 × 17 × 17 grid.

The slice excludes solid cells. Each streamline follows one field snapshot.
The example uses meters and a demonstration viscosity of 0.1 m²/s. Atmospheric validation remains open.
